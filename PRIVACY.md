# Privacy Policy — Daily Dose Auto Upload

Last updated: September 2026

This application is a personal, automated tool operated by the owner of the Daily Dose YouTube channel. It exists solely to upload short-form videos to that channel on the owner's behalf.

## What data it accesses

The app uses Google's YouTube Data API with the owner's own Google account. It requests only the permission needed to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not access, collect, or store personal data from any other users.

## How data is used

The app's access is used exclusively to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel on an automated schedule. No data is shared with any third party. No end-user data is collected, because the app has no end users other than the owner.

## Data retention

The app stores no personal data. Authentication tokens are kept only as a secure secret used to upload to the owner's channel, and can be revoked at any time from Google Account security settings.
